How to Register as a Seller on Wingastock

Becoming a seller on Wingastock allows you to advertise and sell your products to customers through our online marketplace. Follow the steps below carefully to create your seller account.

  1. Read the Seller Terms and Conditions: Before starting the seller registration process, carefully read and understand the Wingastock Seller Terms and Conditions. These terms explain your responsibilities as a seller, including product listings, customer service, payments, prohibited products and compliance with applicable laws.
  2. Start Seller Registration: Select the option to register or become a seller on Wingastock and begin the seller registration process.
  3. Fill in Your Seller Information: Provide the required seller information accurately and completely. Depending on the information requested by Wingastock, this may include your name, business information, contact details and other information required to create and manage your seller account.
  4. Review Your Information: Before continuing, carefully check all the information you have entered. Make sure your details are correct because this information may be used to manage your seller account and communicate with you.
  5. Complete the Seller Declaration: Read the seller declaration carefully and confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and that you agree to the applicable Seller Terms and Conditions.
  6. Receive Your @winga.com Seller Email: During the declaration or account-creation process, Wingastock will provide you with a dedicated @winga.com email address for your seller account. Save this email address carefully. This email is important because it is used specifically for your seller login and for accessing your seller account.
  7. Create Your Account Password: Enter and confirm a secure password for your seller account. Choose a password that is difficult for others to guess and do not share it with anyone.
  8. Submit Your Seller Registration: After completing all required information, reviewing the terms and declaration, receiving your @winga.com seller email and creating your password, submit the registration form.
  9. Keep Your Seller Login Details Safe: Your @winga.com seller email and password are your seller account login credentials. Keep them secure and do not share them with other people.
  10. Log In to Your Seller Account: After successful registration, use your assigned @winga.com seller email and account password to log in and access your seller account.
  11. Start Selling on Wingastock: Once you have access to your seller account, you can use the available seller features to create product listings, add product information, manage your products, communicate with customers and manage your selling activities.

Important: Your @winga.com seller email is specifically used for seller account login and access. Make sure you save it somewhere safe immediately after it is provided to you. If you lose your seller login details, use the available Wingastock account recovery or support process.

Quick Guide: Read Seller Terms → Start Registration → Fill Seller Information → Review Information → Complete Declaration → Receive @winga.com Email → Create Password → Submit Registration → Log In → Start Selling.
