How to Send a Deliver Request

Can't find the product you want on WingaStock? Don't worry. You can send us a Deliver request and we will help you get the product.

Before sending your request: Find a clear picture of the product you want. A good product picture helps us understand exactly what you are looking for.

1

Find the product you want

Search for the product online and find a clear picture that shows the product properly. You can use Google Images, Bing Images, Pinterest or another website.

Search Google Images Search Bing Images Search Pinterest
2

Download the product picture

Save the product picture to your phone or computer. Make sure the picture is clear enough to identify the product.

3

Open the Deliver Request page

After you have the product picture, go to the Deliver Request page and start your request.

Open Deliver Request
4

Enter the product details

Tell us as much as possible about the product you want. This makes it easier for us to understand your request.

What should you include?

  • Product picture
  • Product name
  • Product description
  • Quantity you need
  • Preferred colour
  • Size, model or version
  • Any other special instructions
5

Submit your request

Check your information, make sure the picture is attached, then submit the request. WingaStock will receive your request and work on helping you get the product.

We’ll take it from there! Once your request has been submitted, WingaStock will review the information and help you with the next steps.
